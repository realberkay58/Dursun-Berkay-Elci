# Berkay Elçi - Bioengineering Portfolio

A professional academic and research portfolio website built to showcase my projects in bioinformatics, genetic data analysis, and molecular biology. 

🌍 **Live Site:** [https://realberkay58.github.io/Dursun-Berkay-Elci/](https://realberkay58.github.io/Dursun-Berkay-Elci/)

## Featured Research & Projects
* **Orthologous Variant Detection:** A Python-based automated pipeline (Pandas, BioPython) to extract and filter genetic data from ClinVar, Ensembl, NCBI, and WormBase APIs. Includes evolutionary sequence alignment via UniProt to identify and eliminate false-positive variant matches between Human and *C. elegans*.
* **Bioethics Seminar:** "The Right Not to Know: Ethics of incidental findings in DNA research" – an academic presentation analyzing the psychological and ethical dimensions of incidental findings during genetic testing.
* **Inheritance Modeling:** A scientific narrative project focusing on the HTT gene and the transmission of hereditary diseases.
* **Academic Laboratory Reports:** Detailed documentation on cell culture assays, fluid mechanics, and engineering thermodynamics.
